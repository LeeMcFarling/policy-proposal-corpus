# policy-proposal-corpus

This is a collection of policy proposal documents from the Futures Project. Each document is built to be a standalone overview or policy brief to be readable independently and out of context from the rest of the policy corpus. 

## Table of Contents: 

| Document | Domain | Document Type | Overview |
|---|---|---|---|
| [From DevOps to GovOps](./governance/devops-to-govops.pdf) | Regulatory Modernization | Illustrative sample | Applies software-engineering principles to allow lawmakers to optimize regulatory environments for the protection they confer, the speed and ease of doing business, and the costs associated with compliance. |
| [Care Delivery Reform — Overview](./healthcare/care-delivery-overview.pdf) | Healthcare | Overview | Proposes a two-layer healthcare architecture that separates shoppable market-based care from a universal insurance floor for emergencies. It is structured as an overview (detailing which changes need to be made to care delivery writ large) and aims to achieve the same outcomes as a single payer system while avoiding a government takeover and other pitfalls that have sunk healthcare proposals in the past. |
| [Universal Superannuation — Policy Brief](./fiscal-policy/universal-superannuation.pdf) | Fiscal & Retirement Policy | Policy brief | Proposes building a universal, and portable retirement account system, and using it to amortize Social Security's retirement benefits, with a system that reduces costs by over 90%, and puts more money in the pocket of workers by the time they reach retirement even at half the traditional market performance rate. |


## About the Futures Project: 

The Futures Project is a modular policy platform. Reforms are designed to stand on their own while reinforcing shared objectives across the platform.
